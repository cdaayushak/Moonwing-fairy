# Moonwing-forest
A whimsical 3D fantasy game, featuring interactive NPCs and NPC-to-NPC behavior.

Moonwing Forest is a third-person 3D fantasy game created in Unity.

Play as Moonfairy, a fairy princess blessed with magic by the Moon Goddess, and defend Moonwing Forest from assassins seeking the rare magical potion she protects.

## Story

Hidden beneath moonlight lies Moonwing Forest, home of the Moonfairy.

Blessed with magic by the Moon Goddess herself, Moonfairy protects a rare potion born from that magic. Its power has drawn the attention of the wealthy and powerful, who have sent assassins into the forest to take it.

Guided by her Caretaker and surrounded by the magical beings of Moonwing Forest, Moonfairy must protect her home before her Moonlight fades.

> "When this forest grows quiet... we should be afraid."
>
> — The Caretaker

## Gameplay

Explore Moonwing Forest, interact with its inhabitants, and defend the forest using Moonfairy's enchanted bow and arrows.

Moonfairy's magical energy is represented by her Moonlight. Assassins will drain her Moonlight if they reach her.

Defeat all 6 assassins before Moonfairy's Moonlight reaches zero to protect the forest.

## NPC Behavior

Moonwing Forest contains interactive NPCs rather than purely decorative characters.

The Caretaker and Forest Spirit wander through the environment and can interact with each other when they meet. The Caretaker can also speak to Moonfairy during gameplay.

Their dialogue appears within the game using a floating magical dialogue presentation.

## Combat

Moonfairy uses an enchanted bow to defend Moonwing Forest.

Her arrows contain moonlight magic, and each assassin requires multiple hits to defeat. Assassins pursue Moonfairy throughout the forest while the player moves, aims, and attacks.

Defeat all six assassins to complete the game.

## Moonlight

Instead of traditional health, Moonfairy's life and magical strength are represented by Moonlight.

Moonfairy begins with 50 Moonlight.

If her Moonlight reaches zero:

> YOUR MOONLIGHT HAS FADED.
>
> The forest holds its breath. Even moonlight must rest.

The player can select **TRY AGAIN** to restart.

If all assassins are defeated:

> YOU HAVE DEFEATED THE ASSASSINS.

followed by:

> THE FOREST FALLS SILENT ONCE MORE.
>
> Your moonlight endures. The forest can dream again.

The player can select **PLAY AGAIN** to restart.

## Running the Unity Project

### Requirements

- Unity 6.6 (6000.6.3f1)
- Universal Render Pipeline (URP)

### Instructions

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open Unity Hub.
3. Select **Add project from disk**.
4. Select the downloaded `Moonwing-fairy` project folder.
5. Open the project in Unity.
6. Open `Assets/Scenes/MoonwingForest.unity`.
7. Press **Play** in the Unity Editor.

## About the Project

Moonwing Forest began as a simple 3D game assignment and developed into a fantasy game combining interactive NPC behavior, combat, environmental storytelling, and a cohesive moonlit visual style.

The project was developed iteratively, beginning with the core gameplay systems before moving into character design, environmental development, visual effects, UI, dialogue presentation, camera improvements, and final gameplay polish.
